# Getting Started with Xsilent

Xsilent is a privacy-focused Android vault for photos, videos, PDFs and private files.

This guide covers public, user-facing concepts only.

## Install Xsilent

Download Xsilent from its official Google Play listing:

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xsilent.app

Xsilent is free to install, with no required account, no ads and no subscription.

## Initial setup

After installation:

1. Open Xsilent.
2. Follow the on-screen setup flow.
3. Configure your main access PIN.
4. Enable supported biometric access if you want faster entry.
5. Review the available privacy and locking options.
6. Add private content using Android's system pickers or the supported Share option.

Keep your access PIN secure. Follow the application's on-screen guidance regarding PIN recovery and access protection.

## Private content

Xsilent is designed to protect supported private content such as:

- Photos
- Videos
- PDFs
- Private files

Imported files are stored as encrypted copies inside the vault.

## Importing photos and videos

Xsilent uses Android's system Photo Picker to let you choose photos and videos.

To import:

1. Unlock Xsilent.
2. Open the vault where you want to save your files.
3. Tap **+ Add**.
4. Select the photos or videos you want using Android's Photo Picker.
5. Continue with the on-screen import instructions.
6. Verify that your imported files appear in the vault.

Xsilent does not require access to your entire photo gallery. It receives access only to the files you explicitly select.

### Important: Your original files remain in Gallery

Importing creates encrypted copies inside Xsilent.

**Xsilent does not automatically delete the original files from your Gallery.**

If you also want to hide the originals from your Gallery, delete them manually after confirming that the encrypted copies were imported successfully.

You can also use Android's Share function to send supported photos or videos from your Gallery to Xsilent.

## Importing PDFs and documents

Supported PDFs and other documents are selected using Android's system document picker.

Choose the files you want to import and follow the on-screen instructions.

The original files remain at their source location unless you remove them separately.

## Free and Lifetime

### Free

Xsilent includes:

- 30 free imports in the main vault.
- 30 independent free imports in the decoy vault.
- Up to 10 files per import batch.
- Fake PIN and decoy vault.
- Encrypted backup and restore.
- Encrypted Trash.
- Private PDF viewing.
- PIN protection, Quick Lock and automatic locking.
- Security Center.
- No time limit.

Once your free import allowance has been used, your existing files remain accessible.

You can continue viewing, sharing, exporting, deleting and backing up your files. Backup restoration remains available under the applicable recovery rules.

Deleting files does not restore previously consumed free imports.

### Lifetime — one-time purchase

Lifetime provides:

- Unlimited total imports.
- Up to 20 files per import batch.
- Hidden fingerprint unlock.
- Clean Share privacy tools.
- Everything included in Free.
- No subscription or recurring fees.

Google Play displays the exact local price before purchase.

## Privacy controls

Depending on device support and app settings, Xsilent provides:

- PIN-protected access.
- Fake PIN with a separate decoy vault.
- Supported biometric access.
- Quick Lock.
- Automatic locking.
- Screenshot and screen-recording protection where supported.
- Encrypted Trash.
- Encrypted backup and restore.
- Clean Share privacy tools.

Some options are included in Free, while others require Lifetime, as described above.

## Backups

Encrypted backup and restore are included in Free.

Backups can help recover your private files if you change devices or lose access to the original installation.

- Store backup files only in locations you trust.
- Keep your recovery information secure.
- Verify that your backups can be restored.
- Do not share backup files with other people.

A full recovery into an empty vault is not blocked by the Free import allowance. Restoring into a vault that already contains files follows the remaining Free import allowance.

For more information, see [Encrypted Backup](encrypted-backup.md).
